About

I don’t do “AI commentary.” I do structural thinking: where credibility lives, how systems assign authority, and what it takes to make judgment legible — especially for women navigating invisible rules.

How I think

I treat trust as infrastructure: it’s built through clarity, constraints, and accountability. When AI enters the room, I ask what we’re actually delegating — decision rights, not just tasks.

  • Human-centered AI that respects the stakes of real decisions.
  • Trust signals that come from rigor, not neutrality.
  • Women shaping the defaults that shape everyone else.
Operating principle

If the work can’t be explained clearly, it can’t be trusted. Clarity is not simplification — it’s accountability.

What I work on

Frameworks and public work on AI judgment, credibility, governance, and the cultural patterns that decide who is believed.

What I don’t do

No hype. No fear-mongering. No “future of everything” vagueness. I’m interested in what holds up under scrutiny — and what fails quietly when nobody’s watching.
